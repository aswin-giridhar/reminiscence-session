# Session design rules

These rules come from common reminiscence-practice guidance for people living with dementia. They apply to every caption, opener and song choice. They are content rules for the parent Skill, not medical advice.

## Captions

- One or two short sentences, at most 25 words in total.
- Name only people returned by the NAS person albums. Never guess a name, age or relationship.
- Use the capture year only if the photo's metadata returned it. Otherwise leave the year out.
- Use the present tense and warm, concrete words: "Jean is laughing on the beach."
- Never quiz. Do not write "Do you remember…?" or "Who is this?"
- Never correct or test the person. Do not write "This was not your house."
- Leave out anything on the avoid list, even if it is visible in the photo.

## Conversation openers

Write two or three per session. Make them open invitations that cannot be answered wrongly:

- "Tell me about the seaside."
- "What music did you dance to?"
- "That dress looks lovely. What do you think of it?"

## Music window

- Songs released when the person was between 10 and 25 years old (birth year + 10 to birth year + 25). This is the "reminiscence bump", the age range people tend to remember most strongly.
- Songs the journal marks as "Responded well" come first. Songs marked "Distress" are never chosen again.
- At most 6 songs per session. Never auto-play; the carer starts the music.

## Photo choice

- Prefer clear photos of familiar named people and places.
- Never include a photo of anyone or anywhere on the avoid list.
- Use the carer's number. If fewer photos are found, use what was found and say so.

## Memory book layout

```markdown
# Memory session: (theme)
For (person's name) · prepared (date)

## Photos
1. (caption) · picture ID (id)
...

## Songs
1. (title), (artist), (year)
...

## Things to talk about
- (opener)

## How did it go?
(blank: record a voice note afterwards and use Log mode)
```
