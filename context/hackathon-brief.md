# Hackathon brief (digest of the Devpost pages)

## Dates (PT)

- Round 1 submissions close **Nov 15, 23:45 PT** (Nov 16, 07:45 GMT).
- Round 1 judging runs Nov 16–19. Up to 30 finalists are emailed on **Nov 21**.
- Each finalist gets **one** assigned NAS testing day (iDX6011 Pro, 64 GB) between Nov 22 and Nov 28, from 00:00 to 23:59 PT.
- Round 2 is due **Nov 30, 23:45 PT**: the final ZIP plus a public YouTube demo of at most 3 minutes, recorded on the NAS.
- Winners are announced around Dec 6. Prizes: $20k, $10k, $6k, and 2 × $5k Most Creative (top Innovation scores).

## Round 1 deliverables

1. **PDF**, at most 1 MB, answering 10 questions in at most **500 words** in total. Third-party licences, consent declarations and names are excluded from the count.
2. **ZIP**, at most 10 MB both zipped and unzipped, at most 1 MB per file and at most 100 files.
   - `SKILL.md` with frontmatter `name` (lowercase, hyphenated, 2–64 characters) and `description` (at most 1,024 characters).
   - Optional `references/*.md` files, each linked directly from `SKILL.md`.
   - **No** scripts, executables, binaries or manifests. No real credentials or personal data.
   - The package must disclose third-party components and licences, the work done for the Hackathon, and the AI tools used and what they were used for.

## Judging (equal weight)

User Value · Innovation & Differentiation · Technical Quality · AI NAS Fit & Validation. Round 1 is judged from the PDF and ZIP only; having no NAS evidence does not lower the score.

## Rules worth remembering

- The FAQ *recommends* writing the name and PDF answers yourself, without AI. It is a recommendation, not a requirement. Every AI tool must be disclosed.
- Data may go only to Uliya-supported cloud AI. Use synthetic test data. Direct calls to third-party APIs are prohibited.
- Keep the Round 1 materials unchanged after the deadline and do not delete them.
- Eligible countries: India is **excluded**; the UK is included.
